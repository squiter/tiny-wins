SUPPORT

Let’s take the next small step.

Tiny Wins is still in development. Found a problem or have an idea? Get in touch through the project’s repository.

Browse and report issues ↗

You need a GitHub account to open an issue. Issues are public: please don’t share backups, personal information or your habit history.

Where are my habits stored?

On your device. The app works offline and requires no account. Export a backup in Settings before changing devices or uninstalling the app.

Can I download it yet?

Not yet. The first release is planned for iPhone; Android is in development. Store links will appear on the home page when they are available.

How will purchasing work?

The official iPhone app will be a one-time paid download, without subscriptions or in-app purchases. The price is still to be decided.
