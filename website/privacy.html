PRIVACY

Your habits are yours.

Updated October 16, 2026 · development version

This page describes the current Tiny Wins app and this website. It will be reviewed alongside the release version.

Data on your device

Habit names, completion dates, notes and preferences are kept in the app’s local storage. Tiny Wins requires no registration and does not send this history to a server of ours. The app includes no telemetry, advertising or usage analytics.

Backups under your control

You can export a backup and choose where to save it or whom to share it with. This file contains your history: keep it safe. Imports happen on your device. Your chosen file service may have its own privacy practices.

Removing your data

Local data can be removed by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. Exported copies and any system backups need to be managed separately wherever you saved them.

This website

This website stores only your language preference in this browser. It has no analytics cookies, signup forms or trackers added by the project. Fonts and images are served with the page itself. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process access information under its own policy.

Contact

For questions about the app or privacy, visit the support page. GitHub issues are public; don’t post personal information or backup files.
