## English storefront

Name: Tiny Wins

Subtitle: Small steps. Lasting habits.

Promotional text: Build a wall of little victories. Track your habits, keep your history, and make room for consistency over perfection.

Description:

Tiny Wins is a gentle habit tracker for everyday progress. Read a page, take a walk, drink some water — complete a habit and fill a tile.

Choose daily or weekday routines, give each habit its own icon and color, and see the past 14 days at a glance. Explore your week, month and year through a wall of binary victory tiles.

Keep room to breathe with optional streak flexibility. Tolerated gaps have their own gentle marker; only completed days count as wins. A missed day never erases your earlier effort.

Add a short note to a date, inspect your history, and deliberately correct past entries. Archive a habit when you need a break and restore it later.

Your habits stay on your device. Tiny Wins works offline, requires no account, and offers JSON backup export and restore. Choose light or dark appearance and control celebration animations.

One purchase to download. No subscription or in-app purchases. Choose English or Brazilian Portuguese, or follow your device language. Habit names and notes stay as you wrote them.

## Notes for App Review

Tiny Wins is a local habit tracker. It requires no account, login or server. Create a habit with the + button on Today, choose a name and schedule, save, then tap its completion control. The row shows exactly 14 calendar dates; Insights provides week/month/year reports. Tap a historical date to inspect it, then explicitly confirm any completion change. Settings offers JSON export/restore through native system file dialogs. All features are included in the paid download; there is no in-app purchase flow. Please use example data for review.
